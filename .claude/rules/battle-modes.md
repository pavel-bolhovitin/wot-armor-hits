# Battle modes

- Fog of war: in some modes the client does not know an enemy's vehicle until the enemy is spotted (`arena.hasFogOfWarHiddenVehicles`; the players panel shows an unknown vehicle, `UNKNOWN_VEHICLE_NAME`, without a vehicle type). Never assume an enemy's vehicle type is known: keep it unknown until the client tells it, and never give a type learned later to an earlier moment. The one exception is the vehicle catalog (`data/vehicle_catalog.py`): a player's vehicle met without a type gets the type when the client tells it.
- Respawn modes: a player keeps one arena `vehicleID` for the whole battle, through deaths, respawns (also on the same vehicle) and vehicle changes; only the vehicle type under it changes (`arena.vehicles[vehicleID]['vehicleType']`, `arena.onVehicleUpdated`). Checked in 3 battles on 2026-11-14 (`arenaGuiType` 103, `*_fortrush_26` maps), not in other respawn modes.
- So `vehicleID` is a player in the battle, not a vehicle: a vehicle of a player is the pair `vehicleID` and vehicle type (intCD). The mod's data name it `battlePlayerId` (as `battleId` is the client's `arenaUniqueID`); the mod's `vehicleId` is a vehicle type (intCD).
- `vehicleID` is the only key of a player that is unique in a battle; it means nothing in another battle. The name is not unique (the bots of a training range are all named "Танк-мишень" in the Russian client) and the client may not know it (`UNKNOWN_PLAYER_NAME`); `accountDBID` is 0 for bots and for many real players (6–10 of 30 in a random battle). Both are data to show, never keys.
- The same vehicle's compact descriptor comes in two forms, with and without its optional devices (the flags byte after the modules, then the devices' ids: `VehicleDescr.makeCompactDescr`). Seen for the player's own vehicle: the arena's list (`arena.vehicles[vehicleID]['vehicleType']`) has its devices, the vehicle entity's `typeDescriptor` has not; other players' come without devices from both (all 82 debug records up to 2026-11-15). Take a vehicle's descriptor always from one place, the arena's list first and the entity only when the list has none, and the same vehicle has one string (the vehicle catalog does so); descriptors from different places are compared by type (intCD), never as strings.
